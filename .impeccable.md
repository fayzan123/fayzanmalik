## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, technical collaborators, and fellow CS students/builders who land on the portfolio to quickly evaluate Fayzan's background. Mobile visitors are often checking him out after seeing a link in a LinkedIn/Twitter post or a GitHub bio — they want a fast read: who is this, what have they built, is this person legit? Then they scroll.

### Brand Personality
Technical · Builder · Understated-cool. The site should feel like it was made by someone who actually ships — not a designer's interpretation of a developer.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark terminal/hacker. Tight contrast: near-black (`#0a0a0a`) canvas, electric green (`#00ff88`) accent, white text. Space Grotesk for body/headings (clean, slightly geometric), JetBrains Mono for code, labels, metadata, and the interactive CLI terminal in the hero. NO cyan gradients, NO glassmorphism, NO neon glow cards. The aesthetic is precise and restrained — single accent color used sparingly for emphasis only.

Anti-references: bloated agency portfolios, AI-sloppy gradient-text hero sections, "hiring" neon signs, anything that looks like a template.

### Design Principles
1. **Bio-first on mobile** — the name, title, and 2-sentence bio should be scannable above the fold. CTAs follow. Terminal is bonus content, scrolled into.
2. **Terminal stays** — the interactive CLI is the personality differentiator. Keep it on mobile, but keep it shorter so it doesn't dominate.
3. **Restraint over decoration** — one accent color, used for: accent line, timeline markers, project badges, CTA button, links. Never decorative.
4. **Mono for metadata** — dates, labels, tags, and section labels use JetBrains Mono. Prose and headings use Space Grotesk.
5. **Touch-first interactions** — 44px minimum touch targets, no hover-dependent information, full-width CTAs on mobile.
